\begin{abstract}
This round accompanies the 2026-07-27 external curation pass and states
the method-level finding behind its anti-drift rules.  Every budget so
far charged against the exact \(q=4\) Type-II cell of ROUTE-R3C is
\emph{averaging-portable}: its proof uses only the energy identity, norm
interpolation, bilinear estimates uniform over Tao's averaged
Navier--Stokes class, and radial spectral bookkeeping.  By Tao's
finite-time blow-up theorem for an averaged system, no collection of
averaging-portable budgets can prove global regularity.  The
budget-versus-countermodel arms race of the final eight research rounds
is the visible symptom of this barrier, and the empirical split between
those rounds and the durable milestone results coincides exactly with
the portable/non-portable split: the surviving milestones all use local
energy, pressure, or physical-space carrier structure that does not
survive nonlinearity averaging.  A realization obligation is posed
whose resolution decides, either way, whether radial-scalar refinement
of the \(q=4\) cell can ever close.  The process record fixes two
discipline defects found in this audit: the four 2026-07-27 curation
commits were never pushed to either public remote, and their batch
omitted the required round report.  No Navier--Stokes theorem is proved
here, and the Clay problem is unsolved.
\end{abstract}

\section{Epistemic scope}

The imported blow-up theorem is a peer-reviewed result of
Tao~\cite{Tao16}.  The energy-measure theorem cited for contrast is the
peer-reviewed result of Leslie and Shvydkoy~\cite{LS18}.  The
classification of this repository's budgets into averaging-portable and
averaging-breaking is a same-system audit deduction over the recorded
proofs; it awaits external mathematical review.  The observation that
regularity arguments insensitive to nonlinearity averaging cannot close
is Tao's own stated moral; no literature novelty is claimed for
Theorem~\ref{thm:barrier}.  The contribution of this round is the
file-anchored application to the exact \(q=4\) stack, the resulting
freeze semantics, and the realization obligation of
Section~\ref{sec:realization}.

Nothing here is a regularity theorem, a breakdown construction, an
energy-equality theorem, or a resolution of any Clay alternative.

\section{The averaged class and the barrier}

Tao~\cite{Tao16} constructs a bilinear operator \(\tilde B\), an
average of rotated, dilated, frequency-localised components of the
Euler bilinear operator \(B\), such that the system
\begin{equation}
 \partial_tu=\nu\Delta u+\tilde B(u,u),
 \qquad
 \nabla\cdot u=0
 \label{eq:averaged}
\end{equation}
admits Schwartz divergence-free data whose unique mild solution blows
up in finite time.  The operator \(\tilde B\) preserves the energy
cancellation \(\langle\tilde B(u,u),u\rangle=0\), hence the full energy
identity and energy class, and obeys the standard bilinear
(paraproduct, Sobolev, Lorentz-duality) estimates of \(B\) with
uniform constants.

\begin{definition}[averaging-portable budget]
\label{def:portable}
A trajectory inequality is \emph{averaging-portable} if its recorded
proof uses only:
(i) the energy identity and the energy-class norms it controls;
(ii) bilinear estimates on the nonlinearity that hold, with some finite
constant, uniformly over the class~\eqref{eq:averaged}; and
(iii) radial spectral bookkeeping, that is, calculus on pushforwards of
spectral densities under \(\xi\mapsto|\xi|\) and their heat transforms.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[averaging barrier, immediate from~\cite{Tao16}]
\label{thm:barrier}
No collection of averaging-portable budgets implies global regularity
for the three-dimensional Navier--Stokes equations.  More precisely,
any exclusion argument assembled solely from averaging-portable budgets
applies verbatim to the system~\eqref{eq:averaged} and is therefore
consistent with finite-time blow-up along Tao's schedule.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Tao's solution of~\eqref{eq:averaged} lies in the energy class,
satisfies every inequality of Definition~\ref{def:portable} by
construction of \(\tilde B\), and blows up in finite time.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
Theorem~\ref{thm:barrier} does not by itself forbid closing one
\emph{cell}, that is, one exact schedule such as the \(q=4\) record
ledger, by portable budgets: Tao's blow-up follows its own schedule,
and the averaged class might avoid the \(q=4\) cell entirely.  That
question is exactly Obligation O-AVG-001 in
Section~\ref{sec:realization}.
\end{remark}

\section{Classification of the charged q4 stack}

The budgets actually charged against the \(q=4\) cell in the final
eight research rounds, with their recorded proofs, classify as follows.

\emph{Portable.}
(1) The energy identity and half-energy law \(A'=-AL\)
(rounds of 2026-07-25, radial triad spectrum and successors).
(2) \(M=\|u\|_{\wL}\in L^4_t\) by norm interpolation in the energy
class, and the schedule-conditional record clock \(M\in L^s_t\),
\(s<11/2\): pure arithmetic of the chosen ledger.
(3) \(Y=\|\nabla v\|_2\in L^2_t\): the energy inequality.
(4) The weighted transfer budget
\(\mathfrak q\lesssim\nu^{-1/2}\|v\|_2\|F\|_{\dot H^{-1}}\lesssim
\nu^{-1/2}\sqrt d\,MY\)
of the spectral quantile-speed round: Cauchy--Schwarz in frequency plus
Lorentz duality, both uniform over~\eqref{eq:averaged}.
(5) The fixed-energy quantile speed law and its finite charge: calculus
on the radial density, portable by Definition~\ref{def:portable}(iii).
(6) The Gaussian, diagonal, early-reuse, and recycling ledgers: heat
transforms of radial spectra.

\emph{Averaging-breaking, and exactly the durable results.}
(1) The Leslie--Shvydkoy energy-measure input~\cite{LS18} used by the
temporal five-power barrier and the terminal-dimension pincer: its
proof runs through the local energy balance of suitable solutions;
frequency-space rotation and translation in \(\tilde B\) destroy
spatial locality, so no analogue holds over~\eqref{eq:averaged}.
(2) The \(R^{5/2}\) carrier turnover clock: a physical-space statement
about energy in moving balls.
(3) The adjoint-pressure cost bounds of frozen ROUTE-R3B: the averaged
system has no pressure.
(4) Strong-trace and boundary-drift structure.

\begin{proposition}
\label{prop:split}
Every budget charged against the \(q=4\) cell through the round of
2026-07-25 is averaging-portable.  Every milestone-registry result
(rows 2--5) rests on at least one averaging-breaking input.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch, subject to external review]
Inspection of the recorded proofs listed above.  The portable items
use no localisation in physical space, no pressure, and no property of
the exact triad convolution beyond bilinear boundedness and energy
cancellation.  The milestone inputs are anchored: \cite{LS18} for rows
2 and 4, the moving-ball clock for row 3, pressure costs for row 5.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}
\label{cor:futility}
Refinement of the portable stack alone cannot close the \(q=4\) cell
unless the averaged class avoids the cell.  In particular the
countermodel arms race of the final eight rounds was structurally
forced: each portable budget admits a positive radial spectrum
satisfying it, because portable budgets constrain only radial-scalar
functionals that cascade-supporting averaged dynamics can realise.
\end{corollary}

This is the formal content behind the router rules added on
2026-07-27: a new budget cycle must name structure that fails under
cascade averaging (local energy, pressure, or triad
phase/support geometry), and R3C's freeze counter restarted with the
radial-scalar budgets recorded as spent.

\section{Realization obligation}
\label{sec:realization}

\textbf{O-AVG-001 (open).}  Decide whether some system in the averaged
class~\eqref{eq:averaged}, or in a shell-model class preserving the
energy identity and the portable estimates, admits a trajectory whose
radial spectral energy realises the broad-shell profile
\[
 e_\phi(\lambda,s)=\frac{A(s)}{L(s)}\,
 \phi\!\Bigl(\frac{\lambda}{L(s)}\Bigr),
 \qquad
 L(s)=s^{-9/11}\ell(s)^{2/11},
 \qquad
 A=\frac d2e^{-\int_0^sL},
\]
of the spectral quantile-speed round along the \(q=4\) schedule.

If yes: the smooth broad-shell countermodel is promoted from a spectrum
to a genuine dynamics of an energy-identity-preserving system, every
present and future portable budget tolerates it, and radial-scalar
refinement of the \(q=4\) cell is closed permanently, by theorem rather
than by judgment.  If no: the proof of impossibility must exhibit an
obstruction that is itself averaging-portable, hence a new portable
budget of exactly the kind eight rounds failed to find, which would
reopen the cell with a decisive tool.  Either branch is durable.

\begin{conjecture}
\label{conj:realizable}
The broad-shell profile is realisable in a shell-model class preserving
the energy identity and portable estimates; consequently the \(q=4\)
cell cannot be closed by averaging-portable budgets.
\end{conjecture}

Conjecture~\ref{conj:realizable} is not proved.  A realisation would
not be a Navier--Stokes solution and would resolve no Clay
alternative; it would bound the method only.
