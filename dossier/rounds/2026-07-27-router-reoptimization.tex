document.  The complete course remains the entry point when selecting

\section{Routing preservation}

\begin{proposition}
The revised \texttt{AGENTS.md} and \texttt{HANDOFF.md} preserve every
epistemic and process safeguard added by the Fable curation while
strictly reducing the mandatory cold-start surface for the live R3C
cycle.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The revised constitution still requires: explicit Clay alternative
matching; conditional scoping of the repaired 2607 result; separation
of theorem, source claim, computation, and heuristic; same-sentence
schedule labels; a schedule-free route barrier; the three-cycle
velocity-field-realizability gate; averaging-breaking structure for a
new budget; milestone maintenance; ingestion of signed external
reviews; canonical record ownership; one report per commit batch;
validation, public pushes, and exact-SHA verification.

The only tactical clause removed from the constitution is R3C's restart
date and spent radial-scalar state.  Both now appear in the handoff,
which is their unique live owner.  The handoff names M4 directly and
forbids reading the rest of the course during this bounded cycle.
Therefore no safeguard is lost, while the mandatory strategy read falls
from the full course to one named move.
\end{proof}
