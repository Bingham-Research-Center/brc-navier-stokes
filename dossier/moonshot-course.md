# Moonshot course

**Updated:** 2026-07-27 · **Clay status:** unsolved

This is the standing strategy layer: `AGENTS.md` is the constitution,
this file is the course, `HANDOFF.md` is the current tactic. Read it
before choosing route work or starting a multi-session campaign, not on
every bounded cycle. It was written by the 2026-07-27 external pass at
John's direction; revision discipline is at the end.

## Goal stack

**Primary goal:** prove exactly one Clay alternative A--D as stated in
[the target](clay-target.md). Nothing else counts as the moonshot.

**Secondary goal:** bank artifacts colleagues can take and extend:
errata, conditional lemma notes, certified constraint catalogues, model
realizations, reproduced certificates. A banked artifact is one with a
self-contained write-up, a milestone row, and a recorded external-review
state.

Every session must do one of: advance a move below toward the primary
goal, make a bank deposit, or declare itself maintenance. The best
sessions do the first two at once. Non-goals: prose volume, same-system
review theater, exponent refinement inside frozen or portable-saturated
cells.

Honest odds: the probability that this project produces a Clay proof is
small. The course maximizes the chance anyway while guaranteeing the
journey pays: the moonshot sets direction, the bank justifies the time.

## Strategic picture, plainly

1. The equations fight between transport (which sharpens) and viscosity
   (which smooths); at the critical scale the known estimates break even.
2. Tao proved a fake fluid obeying the same global accounting rules
   blows up, so accounting-only ("averaging-portable") arguments can
   never prove regularity by themselves. This repo's q4 stack is a
   candidate operator-relative instance; its strongest results use
   local energy, true pressure structure, carriers, or triad geometry,
   but the exact classification still requires item-by-item transfer.
3. Inversion: every certified exclusion is also a design constraint. A
   budget that survives tells the regularity side what to strengthen and
   tells the breakdown side what any singularity must look like. Both
   sides feed one ledger; neither direction is wasted.
4. The breakdown alternatives C and D admit a smooth admissible force,
   and for a finite-time singularity the required time decay of the
   force is nearly free; the forced torus route is the widest open door
   in the tree.
5. External review is the rate limiter. Same-system checking is
   saturated; unreviewed results do not compound.

## The ten moves

Each move states what, why it points at the prize, the deliverable, a
kill criterion, and what it banks. IDs are stable; cite them in commits.

**M1. Singularity design-spec ledger.** Compile every certified
necessary condition on a hypothetical first singularity (spectral
broadening, five-power clock exclusions, dimension pincer, energy-jump,
enstrophy floors, R3B gates) into one machine-validated record with
exact hypotheses and route provenance. Why: it is the shared interface
of both Clay sides. Deliverable: a records-layer ledger plus a short
catalogue note. Kill: none; this is infrastructure. Bank: the catalogue
note is citable survey material.

**M2. Broad-shell realization, shell rung (O-AVG-001a).** Build an
explicit energy-identity-preserving shell system whose radial spectral
energy follows the broad-shell profile along the q4 schedule. Why: if it
exists, it proves compatibility with the finite budget stack explicitly
verified on that model; if it cannot, diagnose whether the obstruction is
model-specific. Deliverable: analytic construction first, with an
exact-rational certificate only for a nontrivial finite claim. Kill: three
failed model designs freeze the rung. Bank: bounded method-barrier note.

**M3. Broad-shell realization, averaged rung (O-AVG-001b).** Promote
the shell realization into Tao's averaged class, or prove that class
avoids the q4 cell. Why: upgrades the freeze from judgment to theorem
only if an actual averaged trajectory reaches the cell, and connects
this repo to the modern programme (averaged blow-up)
closest to alternative-C machinery. Deliverable: construction or
exclusion note; nonrealizability alone is not a portable charge. Kill:
three failed embedding strategies freeze the
rung, keeping the shell result. Bank: a note extending a landmark paper
is the most legible artifact this repo could produce.

**M4. Non-portable angular/triad cycle (ROUTE-R3C, at most three
cycles).** The HANDOFF plan, restricted to averaging-breaking inputs:
exact triad convolution support and phase constraints, not cap
counting, against dynamically broad q4 descent. Why: the only live
regularity-side offensive on the active route. Deliverable: a charge
theorem or a velocity-field-realizable survivor. Kill: the standing
three-cycle freeze rule. Bank: any schedule-independent charge gets a
milestone row.

**M5. Forced-cascade feasibility map (ROUTE-B2 probe).** Re-derive the
certified budgets with an admissible force term (obligation: the
current budgets assume no force), then determine which design-spec
constraints a smooth force can pay and which are force-invariant. Why:
this is the exact boundary of the widest Clay door. Deliverable: a
force-sensitivity column in the M1 ledger. Kill: if every constraint is
force-invariant, B2 narrows to B1 and says so. Bank: the sensitivity
analysis is a paper-grade organizing result.

**M6. Torus Pell chaining under admissible force.** Attempt finite-N
chained Pell-triad cascades on the torus with smooth force, each stage
certified in the exact-rational lab, growth tracked against the M1
spec. Why: this is the construction experiment the repo's machinery
uniquely enables; alternative D is its target. Deliverable: certified
stages plus, at each failure, a new necessary condition fed back to M1.
Kill: a certified obstruction that no admissible force can pay; that
obstruction is itself a durable theorem-candidate. Bank: every stage
and every obstruction.

**M7. R3B gate re-triage under portability.** Classify the five frozen
R3B gates as portable or averaging-breaking; strike portable ones from
future intent (they cannot close a regularity route alone), keep the
pressure and Dini physical gates. Why: prunes a large frozen tree
cheaply and prevents a future session resuming a dead line. Deliverable:
one status edit per gate with rationale. Kill: single session; timebox.
Bank: none direct; hygiene.

**M8. Pincer-to-direction bridge.** From the verified terminal geometry
(dimension between three-fifths and one, length-measure zero), attempt
partial vorticity-direction coherence on the singular slice, feeding
the missing antecedent of the repaired 2607 conditional theorem. Why:
the only current line where two verified artifacts compose toward an
unconditional statement; direction geometry is averaging-breaking.
Deliverable: a conditional coherence lemma or a countermodel.
Kill: three-cycle rule. Bank: conditional note plus the Grujic
correspondence channel.

**M9. HWY certificate reproduction (ROUTE-B3A/B3B).** Reproduce the
2509.25116 certificate independently in the exact-rational lab, then
give an honest verdict on the smooth-prehistory or admissible-force
bridge. Why: if the bridge exists it is alternative C or D directly; if
not, the repo owns the reasons. Deliverable: reproduction report plus
bridge verdict. Kill: irreproducible certificate gets logged as such
and the route note updated. Bank: independent reproductions are
immediately citable by others.

**M10. External-review pipeline (standing).** Every verified milestone
ships as a self-contained note to named human experts or a preprint
server (John executes contact and posting); review states tracked in
the registry; target at least one human PDE reader per note. Why: the
rate limiter for everything above. Deliverable: continuous. Kill:
none. Bank: this move is the bank's teller window.

round report. Speed realism: one deep session closes about one
obligation; Gate 0 is four to five sessions; the spine is months of
sessions, not days. The moonshot has no deadline; the bank pays
continuously.

## Recursion dangers: run these checks every session

The two failure modes already observed here are numerology drift
(mistaking arithmetic of chosen constants for equation-forced
structure) and the meta-work spiral (curation and audit replacing
mathematics). A recursive agent rarely fails by wrong algebra; it fails
by a slowly rotating goal. These checks exist to catch the rotation.

**Pre-flight (before any work):**

- **Telescope check.** Write the chain from today's task to a route
  node to a Clay alternative in at most three links. No honest chain,
  no session; re-read this file instead and pick a move.
- **Portability check.** Name the averaging-breaking ingredient, or
  explicitly label the session method-bounding or banking.
- **Session label.** Declare primary-serving, bank deposit, or
  maintenance. More than two consecutive maintenance sessions is a
  spiral; stop and pick a move.
- **State sync.** Fetch and compare both remotes, run the validators,
  then resume the first `NEXT` item in `HANDOFF.md`. Follow only the
  proof note, record, and course anchor named there; do not replay
  `DONE` setup or re-read the dossier.

**Tripwires (during work):**

- A new exponent from a chosen constant gets the schedule-conditional
  label in the same sentence.
- A countermodel is a method result, never route progress; check
  velocity-field realizability before drawing route conclusions.
- Improving a document without closing an obligation is prose quota;
  stop.
- No unverified external claim enters a proof; source record first.
- Long effort on one sub-lemma with no ledger delta: checkpoint into
  `HANDOFF.md` and end the session small rather than thrash.
- Work not named by a possibility-tree node: add the node first or
  stop.

**Post-flight (before commit):**

- Name the obligation or node that changed; if none, label the round
  negative or method-bounding explicitly.
- Existing mechanics: validators green, whitespace clean, round report
  compiled, commit, push both remotes, verify SHAs.
- Schedule-independent result: milestone row in the same commit.
- Replace, never append, `HANDOFF.md`, naming exactly one next bounded
  cycle.
- Bank sweep: if a colleague could take something from this session,
  its write-up and registry row exist now, not later.
- Honesty invariants: Clay unsolved on every surface; any use of
  "solution" matched to exact alternative A--D.

Do not Goodhart the checklist: passing every check while the
mathematics stands still for five sessions means the course itself is
stale. Escalate to John rather than continue.
