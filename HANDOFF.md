## Live implication

At first-record Type-II carriers, canonical defect scales already have
logarithmic residence. For two fixed physical subgrid-energy quantiles,
consecutive selected records now satisfy

\[
r_{j+1}^{\mathrm L}\le r_j^{\mathrm H}
\quad\text{or}\quad
t_{j+1}-t_j
\gtrsim
\frac{\tau_j\ell_j^{\mathrm H}}
{q_j^3\log(e+C/(q_j^2\ell_j^{\mathrm H}))}.
\]

The first branch preserves an amount, not packet identity; the second is a
summable clock. An exact \(q_j=4\), bounded-width ledger makes residence,
clock, and worst weak-\(L^3\) occupation charges all summable. It is not NSE.
